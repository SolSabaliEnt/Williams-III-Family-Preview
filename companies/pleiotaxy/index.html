Proprietary venture.

Pleiotaxy is a wholly family-owned Williams III venture built around a practical tax workflow: individuals can prepare and file with the level of assistance they need, while tax professionals can operate and manage a practice through the same system.

The product is designed to connect return preparation, diagnostics, review, firm workflow, and the operating responsibilities surrounding EFIN- and PTIN-based tax work. The objective is not technology for its own sake, but a clearer system for both independent filers and professionals responsible for serving others.

Within Williams III, Pleiotaxy represents proprietary software developed around a regulated, recurring real-world process. The Family Office provides long-term ownership and strategic stewardship while the venture builds the product, operating model, and infrastructure required to perform reliably over time.
