Strategic interest.

MTC — Make The Crate — is represented within Williams III Family Holdings as a strategic interest in music opportunity infrastructure. It connects independent creators with working curators and tastemakers who can listen, evaluate, organize, and extend legitimate opportunities around music.

The platform is built around a verifiable progression from submission to consideration: submitted, heard, rated, crated, opportunity, and proof. The value is not simply feedback; it is creating a credible operating path between creators seeking access and curators responsible for making real cultural and commercial decisions.

Within Williams III, MTC sits on the strategic-ownership side of the enterprise. Family Holdings provides the institutional context for that interest while the company’s own creator, curator, and opportunity ecosystem remains the operating center of the business.
