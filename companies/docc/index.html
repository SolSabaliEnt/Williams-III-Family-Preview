Strategic interest.

DOCC — Deck of Credit Cards — is represented within Williams III Family Holdings as a strategic interest in consumer financial organization. The product treats each credit card as an ongoing relationship with a purpose, maintenance requirements, and decisions that should remain visible over time.

Its core system is built around the user’s Deck: what they have, why they keep it, what needs attention, and what the product can help monitor or handle. Rather than operating as a digital wallet or spending dashboard, DOCC is intended to reduce the mental overhead of maintaining multiple card relationships responsibly.

Within Williams III, DOCC fits the Family Holdings mandate as an interest in a practical consumer operating layer. Williams III participates through Family Holdings rather than the wholly owned Family Office portfolio.
