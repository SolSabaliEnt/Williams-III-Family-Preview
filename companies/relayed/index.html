Proprietary venture.

Relayed is a wholly family-owned Williams III venture built around freight status automation. Instead of asking drivers, dispatchers, brokers, and customers to repeatedly chase the same information, Relayed interprets load movement and turns it into verified status updates delivered to the people who need them.

Its operating idea is simple: tracking shows where something is; useful communication explains what that movement means. Verified milestones, ETA changes, delay conditions, proof, and communication pressure are organized so the shipment can speak for itself and unnecessary check calls can disappear.

Within Williams III, Relayed reflects a broader pattern of identifying operational friction and building the system required to remove it. The Family Office provides proprietary ownership and long-term stewardship while the venture develops a calmer, more accountable communication layer for freight operations.
