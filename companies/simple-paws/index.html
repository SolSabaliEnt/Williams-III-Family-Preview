Proactive care infrastructure.

Simple P.A.W.S. is designed around continuity: one system for pet identity, records, reminders, household care, and practical guidance across the life of a pet.

The product helps replace fragmented paperwork, memory, calendars, and disconnected portals with a more durable care layer. Its value is not simply storing information; it is helping households understand what matters, remember what comes next, and act before care becomes reactive.

Within Williams III, Simple P.A.W.S. is represented through Family Holdings as a strategic interest in pet technology and consumer health infrastructure. The company extends the enterprise into a recurring consumer relationship with the potential to deepen through longitudinal care, household participation, and trusted digital guidance.
