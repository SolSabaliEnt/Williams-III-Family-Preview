Letter to Shareholders

August 2025

Family Holdings exists to represent Williams III in shared opportunities with discipline, patience, and a long view. Our role is not to pursue ownership for its own sake, but to participate where alignment, utility, and durable value are clear.

We approach every relationship with the same principles: protect what matters, understand the operating reality, and remain selective about where our capital, reputation, and attention are committed.

That means some opportunities will be passed over. Others will be held through cycles. The objective is consistency of judgment rather than activity for activity’s sake.

As the portfolio develops, our standard remains unchanged: relationships should be understandable, incentives should be aligned, and the underlying enterprise should be capable of enduring beyond a short-term moment.

Williams III Family Holdings is built to compound trust as deliberately as capital.
